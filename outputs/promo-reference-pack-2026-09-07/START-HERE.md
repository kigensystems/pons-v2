# Plum film reference images

Seven core images, plus one optional mood reference. Gathered and visually inspected on 7 September 2026. Image 04 was revised at the user's request: the pool was rejected and replaced with an ominous night-woods frame. Open `index.html` to browse; attach the individual images numbered 01–07 to the next creative session. Add 08 if you want to emphasize the dreamier, unsettling side.

## Give the next session this instruction

These images accompany our Plum film brief: **a strange little film about the world Plum connects, with the computer somewhere inside it. Late-night television in 1994, caught between channels.** Ordinary places, obsolete machines, small human moments; disconnected images gradually suggest something working underneath them. Mysterious, nostalgic, hazy, dreamy and gently unsettling, then familiar. Express infrastructure through signals, responses, handoffs and continuing ordinary life, without inventing product claims.

For the dark fragments, lean further into **1980s–90s serial-killer horror suspense and Stranger Things-like ominousness**: near-black woods, cold blue haze, distant flashlight beams, obscured sightlines, and the sense of being watched. Image 04 anchors that feeling. Let it sit beside the warmer domestic images; the film still resolves into familiarity with Plum. The pool is no longer a reference or proposed shot.

Use 01–02 for Plum's identity, 03 for optical softness and faded corporate-brochure atmosphere, 04–06 for the world and human feeling, and 07 as the exact end-card mark. The Macintosh is incidental set dressing. The site screenshots do not prescribe a hardware-led film. Develop original scenes from the light, texture, framing and emotional qualities of these references. Aim for early-1990s commercial footage, local broadcasts and imperfect family tapes, with restrained grain, diffusion and analog softness. The imagery may feel messy and loosely related; sound and recurring responses can connect it. End with the supplied mark and **Plum**, then **a familiar feeling.**

These are visual references, not a locked storyboard. The Larry Sultan photographs span 1986–1991. Image 04 is a 2016 television frame with a retro horror atmosphere, not archival 1980s–90s footage. Keep the next film's props and wardrobe consistent with its chosen period. No final model, running time or format is implied by this pack.
