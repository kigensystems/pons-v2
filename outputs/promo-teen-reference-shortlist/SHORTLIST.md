# Plum: teenage reference shortlist

These are four proposed references for discussion, not approved replacements. The user rejected the previous web-image selections as insufficiently close to Plum and Shader, including both the pool and the Stranger Things woods, and clarified that teenagers would fit better.

The target is teenage small-town unease with faded commercial / analog image qualities: warm cream highlights, subdued blue and green, soft focus, haze, tactile ordinary surroundings, casual human behavior, and a familiar place that feels a little wrong. Match texture and light as well as subject. The film remains about the world Plum connects; devices sit within everyday life. It still ends with Plum and a familiar feeling. No test videos or frames from the ten-second generations are included.

## Four proposed images

1. **`elm-street-phone-candidate.jpg` — A Nightmare on Elm Street (1984), bedroom telephone conversation.** Best candidate here for warm lamp bloom beside a dark window, muted blue, cream and mauve, soft detail, and unease inside an ordinary room. [Source video page](https://www.dailymotion.com/video/x5txhop); [unchanged poster JPEG](https://s2.dmcdn.net/v/L14Z91ep9BFvBe31Z/x480). This is the publicly supplied still/poster, not a downloaded video. Director: Wes Craven. Film rights: respective production rightsholders; no production reuse license established.
2. **`twin-peaks-home-video-candidate.jpg` — Twin Peaks pilot (1990), Laura and Donna's picnic tape.** Reference the teenage friends and the faded, smeared picture inside the television. The TV casing is not a proposed hero object or shot composition. [Source article by Steven Miller, Twin Peaks Blog](https://twinpeaksblog.com/2024/09/18/costuming-peaks-donna-hayward-in-the-twin-peaks-pilot/); [unchanged JPEG](https://twinpeaksblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/TPB_Costume_DonnaHaywardS1_Pilot07.jpeg). Series creators: David Lynch and Mark Frost. Rights: respective Twin Peaks production rightsholders; no production reuse license established.
3. **`virgin-suicides-phone-candidate.jpg` — The Virgin Suicides (1999), teenage friends sharing a receiver.** Reference the soft brown-and-cream image, unperformed closeness, and communication as a small human act. This film is set in the 1970s; borrow its light and emotional register while keeping Plum's own chosen 1980s–90s period. [Source article, Funk's House of Geekery](https://houseofgeekery.com/2020/10/08/classic-scene-telephone-call/); [unchanged JPEG](https://houseofgeekery.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/class1.jpg?w=1024). Director: Sofia Coppola. Film rights: respective production rightsholders; no production reuse license established.
4. **`twin-peaks-school-candidate.jpg` — Twin Peaks pilot (1990), school corridor.** Reference teenage everyday life, cream institutional surfaces, worn clothing, subdued light and a caught-between-moments composition. [Source article by Steven Miller, Twin Peaks Blog](https://twinpeaksblog.com/2024/09/18/costuming-peaks-donna-hayward-in-the-twin-peaks-pilot/); [unchanged JPEG](https://twinpeaksblog.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/TPB_Costume_DonnaHaywardS1_Pilot01.jpeg). Rights as above.

The proposed combination is 1 for ominous lighting, 2 for faded image texture, and 3–4 for teenage human presence. Develop original people and scenes; these are visual references, not a request to recreate the films or their stories. None has yet received the user's visual approval.
