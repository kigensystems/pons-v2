# Opening to Explore

September 7, 2026. The scroll-driven zoom from the opening into Explore was removed at the user's request after two revisions still broke in the in-app browser: "remove scroll entirely. We will just have a simple enter that matches the aesthetic."

The opening is a single non-scrolling scene again. An Enter link in the intro, styled as an ivory keycap beside a Garamond label, navigates to `/explore`. The masthead kept links to Explore and About until the September 7 night polish removed both; Enter is now the only way out of the opening. Explore and About are standalone routes; `/launch` remains an Explore alias.

No transition code, runway, or portal remains. The TV, lighting, and renderer are exactly as the opening renders them.
