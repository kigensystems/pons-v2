# Ten more launched-token website references

Current desktop websites inspected September 6, 2026. These are ten additional references, giving fifteen across the session. This is a curated visual comparison, not an exhaustive ranking or evidence that design caused token success. Current designs are not verified launch-era designs.

**Start with EigenCloud, Ethena, Pyth, Ondo and Sui.** The other five broaden the options: Sei for institutional photography, Pendle for product separation, Render for creative outputs, Akash for workflows, and Bittensor for scientific minimalism.

## Sui — SUI

[Live site](https://sui.io/) · [Hero](screenshots/sui.png) · [Full-page attempt](full-page-screenshots/sui-stitched.png)

**Observed:** Electric blue radial slab tunnel behind oversized white typography; black-and-white editorial sections; modular stack diagrams and audience paths.

**Assessment:** A strong cinematic entry with an unusually concrete infrastructure stack underneath.

**Borrow:** Pair one memorable spatial metaphor with a clear modular explanation.

**Token role:** Layer-1 infrastructure for builders and autonomous systems. Native SUI pays gas and supports staking/governance.

**PONS application, proposed:** Show independent PONS launch tools as a connected stack beneath a distinctive hero.

**Counterpoint:** Heavy motion and large scroll distances could hurt clarity and low-powered devices.

**Launch evidence:** Official 90-day retrospective confirms Sui mainnet launched May 3, 2023; native SUI network token. [Source](https://www.sui.io/blog/mainnet-90-day-metrics-peaks).

**Supplemental official sources:** [Source 1](https://docs.sui.io/develop/sui-architecture/tokenomics-overview).

**Capture limits:** Full-page composite has a marked 95px scroll gap, 2px uncaptured tail, repeated fixed promotion, and motion/lazy-load seams; suitable for layout reference only.

## Sei — SEI

[Live site](https://www.sei.io/) · [Hero](screenshots/sei.png) · [Full-page attempt](full-page-screenshots/sei-stitched.png)

**Observed:** Full-bleed green-gold city photography, condensed uppercase hero, restrained burgundy CTAs, alternating white editorial and dark data sections.

**Assessment:** Best added reference for institutional confidence and connecting abstract infrastructure to real economic activity.

**Borrow:** Use real-world imagery and editorial typography to make infrastructure feel established.

**Token role:** Trading-focused L1; native SEI gas, staking and governance.

**PONS application, proposed:** Present a PONS infrastructure product with precise operational screens and architecture-led photography.

**Counterpoint:** Hero could belong to a financial company; differentiation relies on the lower network story. Giga performance must be separated from current deployed capacity.

**Launch evidence:** Sei Labs release dated August 15, 2023 confirms mainnet live; current official staking docs describe native SEI network staking. [Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sei-launches-mainnet-301900263.html).

**Supplemental official sources:** [Source 1](https://docs.sei.io/learn/general-staking), [Source 2](https://docs.sei.io/evm/precompiles/governance), [Source 3](https://docs.sei.io/learn/sei-giga).

**Capture limits:** Static stitch may show animation transitions; site performance and network metrics not validated.

## Pyth Network — PYTH

[Live site](https://www.pyth.network/) · [Hero](screenshots/pyth.png) · [Full-page attempt](full-page-screenshots/pyth-stitched.png)

**Observed:** Black background with multicolour radial data bars, thin white type, softly lit dark cards, four compact growth charts and a purple closing section.

**Assessment:** One of the strongest references for making infrastructure tangible through visible data and developer entry points.

**Borrow:** Let the product's data supply the visual identity and the proof below the hero.

**Token role:** Oracle/data network. PYTH governance and oracle-integrity collateral staking; not the required currency for ordinary feed payments.

**PONS application, proposed:** A PONS tools site could preview launch activity, graduation progress and integration examples with clearly sourced data.

**Counterpoint:** Glowing cards and award badges add clutter; some live charts loaded incompletely in capture.

**Launch evidence:** Official 2023 retrospective confirms successful November PYTH airdrop and token-led governance launch. [Source](https://www.pyth.network/blog/where-pyth-is-now-looking-towards-2024).

**Supplemental official sources:** [Source 1](https://docs.pyth.network/pyth-token), [Source 2](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/current-fees).

**Capture limits:** Some dynamic price charts remained partially drawn; static capture does not establish live feed reliability.

## Ondo Finance — ONDO

[Live site](https://ondo.finance/) · [Hero](screenshots/ondo.png) · [Full-page attempt](full-page-screenshots/ondo-stitched.png)

**Observed:** White editorial canvas, nested rounded city-image frames splitting the hero headline, serif supporting copy, pastel product charts, black trust/technology panels and oversized footer wordmark.

**Assessment:** Excellent premium finance reference: memorable composition without relying on neon or science-fiction styling.

**Borrow:** Use restrained type contrast, architectural framing and product-specific visual evidence.

**Token role:** Tokenized finance platform; ONDO governs Ondo DAO/Flux Finance, not a required payment token for these products. Official token docs confirm restrictions lifted January 18, 2024.

**PONS application, proposed:** A polished PONS infrastructure brand could use a calm light theme and clear tool-specific data cards.

**Counterpoint:** Long page and persistent disclosure obscure parts of the design; governance token is a weaker product-utility match.

**Launch evidence:** Exchange initial listing dated January 18, 2024 specifies ONDO trading; official Ondo Foundation describes token sale and governance allocation. [Source](https://www.mexc.com/announcements/article/initial-listing-mexc-will-list-ondo-foundation-ondo-in-assessment-zone-17827791512880).

**Supplemental official sources:** [Source 1](https://docs.ondo.foundation/ondo-token), [Source 2](https://docs.ondo.foundation/ondo-dao).

**Capture limits:** Persistent disclosure overlay obscures left portions of full-page composite; no acknowledgment or terms accepted. Hero navigation was still transitioning in initial capture.

## Render Network — RENDER

[Live site](https://rendernetwork.com/) · [Hero](screenshots/render.png) · [Full-page attempt](full-page-screenshots/render-stitched.png)

**Observed:** Moving GPU-render showreel in hero; white lower canvas; vivid credited artist work and monochrome sculptural art; rounded benefit cards and red accents.

**Assessment:** Best reference here for demonstrating a creative infrastructure service through the work it enables. Lower page is conventional.

**Borrow:** Show compelling outputs enabled by the infrastructure.

**Token role:** GPU rendering/compute network. Solana RENDER replaced legacy Ethereum RNDR; completed-job receipts burn RENDER, operators receive emissions, holders vote.

**PONS application, proposed:** Demonstrate launches or useful tools enabled by PONS infrastructure in a curated product reel.

**Counterpoint:** Hero impact depends heavily on third-party artwork; layout alone is less distinctive than the other shortlisted sites.

**Launch evidence:** Official upgrade FAQ confirms existing RNDR and launched Solana RENDER migration, with October 30, 2023 snapshot and upgrade support. [Source](https://know.rendernetwork.com/general-render-network/rndr-to-render-what-you-need-to-know/render-network-upgrade-portal-faq).

**Supplemental official sources:** [Source 1](https://renderfoundation.com/faq).

**Capture limits:** Video stitched at different frames; one-pixel uncaptured tail. Optional cookies declined before final capture.

## EigenCloud — EIGEN

[Live site](https://www.eigencloud.xyz/) · [Hero](screenshots/eigencloud.png) · [Full-page attempt](full-page-screenshots/eigencloud-stitched.png)

**Observed:** Huge bold white headings, softly shaded blue/purple block sculptures, monospace navigation and supporting text, dramatic oversized serif footer on deep violet.

**Assessment:** One of the strongest direct infrastructure art-direction references: sculptural modules map onto a product stack.

**Borrow:** Give each infrastructure component a coherent object within one visual family.

**PONS application, proposed:** Represent PONS tools as a small family of distinct, connected modules with actual feature explanations.

**Counterpoint:** Animation and dark low-contrast supporting copy can hide the practical product explanation.

**Launch evidence:** Official Eigen Foundation October 1, 2024 announcement confirms EIGEN unlocking and transferability. [Source](https://blog.eigenfoundation.org/unlock-eigen/).

**Supplemental official sources:** [Source 1](https://www.eigenlabs.org/blog/introducing-eigencloud/), [Source 2](https://blog.eigenfoundation.org/unlock-eigen/).

**Capture limits:** Browser reported invalid 1x1 geometry. Native full-page capture is defective with large gaps/repetition and unusable as layout evidence. Hero and separately inspected scroll frames support observations; do not use full-page output as faithful reference.

[Live site](https://pendle.finance/) · [Hero](screenshots/pendle.png) · [Full-page attempt](full-page-screenshots/pendle-stitched.png)

**Observed:** Large fine-wire sphere on black, rotating yield verbs, blue and teal orbital illustrations for two product branches, thin outlined controls and dark statistic cards.

**Counterpoint:** Thin lines and low-contrast type can reduce accessibility; animated counters and logos captured before fully settling.

**Launch evidence:** Official tokenomics records circulating PENDLE and full team/investor vesting by September 2024, confirming launched token. [Source](https://docs.pendle.finance/pendle-v2/ProtocolMechanics/Mechanisms/Tokenomics).

**Supplemental official sources:** [Source 1](https://docs.pendle.finance/pendle-v2/ProtocolMechanics/Mechanisms/sPENDLE).

## Scope and validation

All ten have documented existing tokens; token roles differ. ONDO, ENA and PENDLE must not be described as required payment currencies for their associated products. RENDER is distinguished from legacy RNDR; current sPENDLE replaces winding-down vePENDLE. Exact original launch dates were not established for TAO and PENDLE. ENA public launch and vesting-start dates are distinct. Sei Giga performance is not confirmed current mainnet capacity.

Ten heroes were inspected. Nine sites have reconstructed full-page or one-viewport captures with noted limitations. EigenCloud has a defective native full-page attempt; its hero and inspected scroll frames are usable. Structural validation passed for ten journaled candidates and two checkpoints. No product, mobile, performance, financial claims or conversion testing was performed. PONS integration and demand remain unverified as described in the first report.
